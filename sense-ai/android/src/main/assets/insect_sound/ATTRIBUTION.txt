The Android frontend and hierarchical postprocessing are ports of src/audio.py
and src/inference.py at the pinned revision (MIT; Copyright (c) 2026 Kernel MVP
contributors; see LICENSE). Changes: Kotlin implementation, bounded PCM16 WAV
input, Android LiteRT interpreter, strict quality rejection rather than emitting
scores on failed audio, and unknown identification represented with no label.
No Python, HDF5 or pickle code executes in the shipped app.

Experimental closed-set audible sound classification of 32 species only
(9 Orthoptera and 23 Cicadidae). Not grain-pest screening, proof of absence,
ultrasound sensing, or a basis for treatment/lot decisions. Scores are not
calibrated. Numerical port parity does not establish field accuracy or mobile
readiness across devices. The upstream card documents substantial limitations.
